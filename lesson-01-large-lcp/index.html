Lesson 01 · Largest Contentful Paint

This hero image weighs more than the rest of the site combined.

Orbit Studio is a fictional design agency landing page. Its hero photo is a 4200×2800, ~9.6MB JPEG loaded with no compression, no dimensions, and no priority hints — the single worst thing you can put above the fold.

Why this page scores so badly

Open DevTools → Network, throttle to "Fast 3G" or "Slow 4G", and reload. Watch hero-image.jpg dominate the waterfall — it alone can take longer to arrive than an entire well-built page. Then run Lighthouse and check the LCP element: it will point straight at this image. Nothing else on this page is intentionally broken — compare it to a later lesson to see how isolated the fix would be.
